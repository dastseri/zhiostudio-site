NOIR ESSENCE

A fragrance you step into — not a page you scroll past.

One scent, told the way it unfolds on skin: a spark, a heart, a depth. Scroll, and the notes arrive in order.

Chapter I — Top Note

The Spark

Calabrian bergamot, cut with light.

The first breath of NOIR ESSENCE is green gold — bergamot peel warmed by sun, bright enough to turn a head in the first three seconds. Like a homepage, the opening note has one job: make you stay.

Bergamot · Green Leaves · Morning Light

Chapter II — Heart Note

The Heart

Night jasmine over Damask rose.

Then the fragrance turns to velvet. Jasmine that only opens after dark, folded into rose petals the colour of sealed wine. This is the chapter visitors remember — the one they describe to someone else.

Night Jasmine · Damask Rose · Velvet Petals

Chapter III — Base Note

The Depth

Amber, oud, and a trace of smoke.

Hours later, what remains is warmth: fossilised amber lit from inside, dark oud wood, a rumour of incense. The base note is the reason the fragrance is still there at midnight — and the reason this story ends with a bottle, not a banner.

Golden Amber · Oud Wood · Incense Smoke
