The Speaker

Mindset & wellness expertise, earned across two decades of change

Vaishali Joshi is a Mental Health Speaker and Workplace Wellness Strategist who masterfully blends strategy with mental wellbeing in her work. She draws on a rare 20+ years of experience across eight industries in global business transformation leadership and energy-based mindset training.

A witty and multifaceted storyteller, Vaishali's keynotes leverage her lived experiences as a woman of color, immigrant, corporate executive, (step)parent, mountaineer, classically trained dancer, and youth advocate to make a compelling case to leaders about how to strategize and build wellness-based talent development plans and cultures that thrive sustainably through change.

Vaishali is also the Founder & CEO of oraHuman℠. Since 2023, her signature ORA Experience℠ program has empowered professionals and entrepreneurs to proactively ward off the threat of stress, anxiety, burnout, change fatigue, and disconnection so they can lead and belong to others with clarity and confidence. The mission is to help people build calmer, collaborative, and productive mindsets and communities.

She holds credentials in both business management and mindfulness studies from notable institutions including Schulich School of Business, Queen's University, The Art of Living Foundation, Eckhart Tolle School, Isha Foundation and the Centre for Mindfulness Studies.

The ORA℠ Method

Observe. Release. Activate.

ORA℠ is a proprietary methodology that empowers individuals to apply three powerful mindset levers continually — building the skills to maintain mental and emotional wellness, and to reframe their relationship with stress so they can habitually operate from a place of calmness, empathy and effectiveness.

ora

Three levers, one practiceTouch a lever as it circles — Observe, Release, Activate — and feel how ORA℠ is practised: continually, and gently.

O

Observe

Self-discovery first: identify the triggers behind stress, distraction and disconnection — the sources of power and limitation that shape your goals.

R

Release

Let go of the negativity and anxiety that hijack your response — swiftly, before they lead — and gain awareness and control over limiting beliefs and behavioural patterns.

A

Activate

Channel your energy to the right intelligences at the right time — Vaishali's definition of mental agility. You have much more than IQ and EQ: find the power of your full mind.

“Each of us must learn about the power of the “full mind” and draw positive power from within. This is a sustainable place to come from.” — Vaishali Joshi

About Vaishali

From corporate trailblazer to inspirational adventurer.

Vaishali is a global change leader and mental health speaker who holds multiple designations as a leader in business and human transformation. In 2025, she was nominated for Canada's Women of Influence “Ones to Watch” award, Canada's top mental health advocate award for Workplace Wellness Leadership and Canada's top 25 immigrants award.

She has been featured on Canada's Speaker Slam stage, the Canadian Small Business Entrepreneur podcast, CHCH TV, OMNI TV and multiple magazines including Authority and Canvas Rebel — and formerly, as a trained Indian classical dancer, she has graced Toronto stages such as Dusk Dance, Luminato, IIFA and Nuit Blanche.
