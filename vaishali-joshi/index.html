About Vaishali

From corporate trailblazer to inspirational adventurer.

Vaishali Joshi is a Workplace Wellness Strategist who blends strategy with mental wellbeing. She draws on a rare 20+ years of experience across eight industries in global business transformation leadership and energy-based mindset training.

A multifaceted storyteller, she brings the lived experience of a corporate executive, immigrant, mountaineer and classically trained dancer to a compelling case: how leaders can build wellness-based cultures that thrive sustainably through change. Since 2023, her signature ORA Experience℠ has carried that work to professionals and entrepreneurs.

“Every individual can and must learn the power to discern and draw positive energy from within. That is a sustainable place to come from.” — Vaishali Joshi
