Add AVA to your booking line

Every booking inquiry, answered with care — 24/7

Organizers and HR teams often call after hours, between meetings, from another time zone. AVA — the AI receptionist from NexAIS, the studio behind this concept — answers Vaishali's booking line around the clock, so no inquiry is ever missed or left waiting.

  • Answers 24/7. Every booking-inquiry call is picked up — evenings, weekends, holidays.
  • Captures the complete details. Event date, organization, audience and contact — everything the team needs to reply well, the first time.
  • Follows up. Callers are confirmed and followed up by text and email, so a warm inquiry never goes cold.
  • Hands over with care. Urgent or complex requests are passed straight to a human.

AVA is a separate service you can add alongside the website — commercially, it is a purchasable part of this offer, ready whenever your booking line is.
