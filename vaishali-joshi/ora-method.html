The ORA℠ Method

Observe. Release. Activate.

ORA℠ is a proprietary methodology that empowers individuals to apply three powerful mindset levers continually — to unlock sources of power and limitations that play a part in whatever future goals or aspirations you hold close to your heart.

O

Observe — self-discovery first

Identify the triggers behind stress, distraction and disconnection. ORA begins with seeing clearly: the needs and wants of others can consume us until we lose sight of our true selves — observation is how we take that control back.

R

Release — let it go, swiftly

Let go of the negativity and anxiety that hijack your response — before they lead. Release builds awareness and control over your limited beliefs and behavioural patterns, and makes room for a customized stress reduction practice.

A

Activate — your full mind

Channel your energy to the right intelligences at the right time. “Mental Agility is about knowing how to activate and channel energy to the right intelligences at the right time.” You have much more than IQ and EQ — find the power of your full mind.

The framework behind the levers

Whether you are a working professional, a caregiver to others, or have reached the pinnacle of your career, the flagship ORA Experience℠ — built on this method — is designed to unlock sources of power and limitations that play a part in whatever future goals or aspirations you hold close to your heart.

ORA focuses on reframing your relationship with stress so you can habitually operate from a place of calmness, empathy and effectiveness. It is a 4-step human skill development program, available as a virtual or in-person program.

It is very easy to be consumed by the needs and wants of others. This is how we lose sight of our true selves and feel trapped. And that compounds our stress and anxiety. And that is when we may experience physical depletion and emotions such as anger, sadness, and confusion. And this is how our human spirit stops thriving. The world's most successful people have a daily mental fitness practice — ORA is Vaishali's.
