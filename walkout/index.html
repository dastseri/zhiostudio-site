Three steps. Then the door.

This is a working miniature of the real experience. You drive it: open a tab, add dishes, choose how to pay, and leave.

1

Scan

Every table carries a small QR tent. One scan opens a tab for that table — no app download, no account to create. A card is saved once, securely, to run the tab against.

2

Dine

Order with your server as usual. Each dish lands on your live tab the moment it's ordered, so the total is never a surprise — subtotal, HST and tip, itemised in real time.

3

Choose — then go

Walk-Out Pay: get up and leave; the tab closes itself and the receipt lands in your inbox. Traditional pay: settle at the counter with the card machine or cash, the way you always have. Both are first-class options, side by side, on every tab.

Honest note: there are no cameras, sensors or tracking tricks here. It is a QR code, a saved card and a tab that closes when you say you're leaving — simple technology a real neighbourhood restaurant can actually run.

For the restaurant: the bill wait is the worst seat in the house

The last ten minutes of a meal — flagging a server, waiting for the bill, waiting for the machine — are the minutes guests remember. Walk-Out Dining removes them for the guests who want it gone, and changes nothing for the guests who don't.

⏱️

No bill wait

Guests leave the moment they're ready. The slowest part of the meal disappears, and so does the end-of-meal frustration that colours reviews.

🔄

Faster table turns

Tables free up when the meal ends, not ten minutes later. On a Friday night, those recovered minutes are covers you can actually seat.

🛡️

Fewer walk-outs

Every walk-out tab runs against a saved, verified card. Dine-and-dash stops being a shrug and a write-off — the tab is covered before the first dish fires.

👀

Tabs staff can see

Every open tab, its table and its running total sit on one staff view. Servers see what's been ordered and what's outstanding — no paper bills to chase.

🤝

Traditional pay, untouched

Walk-out is an opt-in, per guest. The counter, the card machine and the cash drawer work exactly as they do today, for every guest who prefers them.

💳

Cards handled by Stripe

Saved cards live with Stripe, not on your systems. The restaurant never stores card numbers — payments run on the same rails as a normal online order.

How it actually works — honestly

A QR tent on each table opens that table's tab in the guest's browser. The guest saves a card once through Stripe; dishes are added to the tab as they're ordered; the guest picks a payment path — walk-out auto-close, or traditional payment at the counter. If they walk out, the saved card is charged and the tab closes. No cameras. No sensors. No ceiling hardware. If your POS can't sync tabs yet, the tab system runs alongside it — your team sees both on one screen.
