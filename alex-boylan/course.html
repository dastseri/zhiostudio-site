The Curriculum

One outcome, five steps

The outcome is specific: land your dream job before graduation. Here's the system that gets you there.

Step 01

Pick the destination

Define the dream job precisely — role, industry, and the life it has to fit — before you apply to anything.

Step 02

Build proof, not just a résumé

Turn classes, projects and part-time work into evidence an employer can see and believe.

Step 03

Tell your story

Craft the personal narrative that makes your miles — every experience so far — add up to one clear pitch.

Step 04

Open doors on purpose

Networking and mentorship as a repeatable system, not luck — who to reach, what to say, how to follow up.

Step 05

Apply & interview to win

Applications, interviews and offers: the tactics that convert preparation into a signed offer.

Step 06

Launch before graduation

Negotiate, start strong, and carry the Playbook into your first year on the job.
