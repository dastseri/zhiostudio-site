Why layers?

A menu you can hold in your hand

A single photo lies politely: it flattens the stack and never shows you how tall a burger really stands. So we photograph every signature dish exactly as the kitchen builds it, in the light it is served in — and let you pull it apart before you commit.

The exploded view is our favourite part. It is the recipe, drawn in space: bun, lettuce, patty, cheese, tomato. No garnishes that exist only for the camera, no “serving suggestion” fine print. If it is in the layers, it is on your plate.
